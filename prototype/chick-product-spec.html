Product specification
& feature brief.

The complete feature set, user stories, and acceptance criteria for the Chick MVP — everything a builder needs to implement, test, and ship.

Problem Statement

Pre-launch founders waste their first 90 days on the wrong channels. They don't have a wrong product — they have no system for finding the right people to talk to. Apollo, Clay, and Apify assume you already know your ICP. Chick is built for the part before that.

The result: founders get their first customer conversation 4–8 weeks faster than going it alone, with community-specific outreach that doesn't get them banned.

Features — MVP Scope

01
Idea Intake
Free-text input. No forms. Founder describes their idea in plain language — rambling is fine. Fastino converts it to a structured ICP hypothesis in seconds.
"As a founder, I want to describe my idea in plain English so I don't have to fill in a structured form before I've validated anything."
  • Text input accepts minimum 10 characters, no maximum enforced
  • Fastino extraction completes within 3 seconds on p95
  • Structured output shows audience hypothesis, product category, stage, tone
  • If extraction confidence <0.6, show 3 follow-up questions before proceeding
  • Founder can edit any structured field before running discovery
02
Community Discovery
Chick searches subreddits, Discord servers, and Facebook groups where the ICP actually lives. Cala verifies every result — no dead communities, no stale member counts.
"As a founder, I want a list of real, active communities where my target customer hangs out, so I'm not wasting outreach on dead channels."
  • Returns minimum 10, maximum 30 verified communities
  • Each community shows platform, member count, and activity score
  • Dead communities (0 posts in 30 days) are filtered before display
  • Results load within 8 seconds on p95
  • Founder can mark any community as "irrelevant" to exclude from kit
03
Confidence Scoring
Every community scored on three axes: Reach (relevant members, not raw count), Receptiveness (mod culture, self-promo rules), Warmth (founder's existing foothold). Top 10 surface first.
"As a founder, I want communities ranked by how likely I am to get a response, not just how big they are."
  • Three scores displayed per community (Reach / Receptiveness / Warmth), each 0–100
  • Composite score used for sort order
  • Founder can re-sort by any individual axis
  • Score methodology shown on hover (no black box)
04
Outreach Kit Generation
For each selected community, Chick drafts the right move for that culture. A 7-day plan, a copy-paste post, a DM template, platform-specific Do's & Don'ts, Week 1 KPIs.
"As a founder, I want ready-to-use outreach copy written for the specific community I'm posting in, not a generic template."
  • Kit generated per community, not one-size-fits-all
  • Post copy respects community tone and mod rules from Cala data
  • DM template distinct from public post
  • 7-day day-by-day plan is actionable (specific actions per day, not vague advice)
  • All copy is copy-paste ready — no placeholder text left in output
  • Kit generates within 10 seconds on p95
05
Tracking & Refinement Loop
Every attempt logged: channel, message variant, outcome. Silence is a signal too. Patterns re-rank communities for the next pass — every loop, the founder gets closer to PMF.
"As a founder, I want to log what happened with each outreach attempt so the system can tell me what's working and adjust my strategy."
  • Outcome options: Yes / No / Interested / Silence
  • Response time logged automatically if founder records date
  • Refinement run triggers automatically after 5+ logged events
  • Re-ranked list shows delta from original ranking (↑↓ indicator)
  • Pattern summary shown in plain language ("Discord converting 3× better than Reddit")

Out of Scope — MVP

The following are explicitly not in scope for the hackathon build and should not be designed around:

Automated posting or DM sending on behalf of the founder · Email outreach tooling · CRM or pipeline management · Multi-founder teams / collaboration · Mobile app · Payment / billing flows · Integrations beyond Fastino and Cala · Analytics beyond the core tracking log
