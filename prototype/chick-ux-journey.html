Founder describes their idea → AI structures the brief → discovers where the audience lives → scores every path → writes the outreach kit → tracks what works → refines the loop.
1
✍️
Idea Intake
Founder describes the idea in plain language
No forms. No structured input. The founder types how they'd explain it to a friend — the idea, their best guess at who it's for, what stage they're at. Rambling is fine. That's the point.
What Chick does
Fastino text-to-JSON converts the description into a structured brief: audience hypothesis, product category, stage, tone. Treated as a hypothesis — not a fact — so the system can challenge and sharpen it.

Discovery
Engine searches everywhere the audience actually lives
For each audience segment, Chick searches Discords, subreddits, Facebook groups, and company lists. Community activity is verified — not just indexed. Dead or low-quality communities are filtered before they reach the founder.
What Chick does
Cala knowledge search supplies verified, sourced company and market data across Agro, Finance, HR, and other verticals — no hallucinated names, no stale lists. Real communities, real member counts, real activity signals.

Confidence Scoring
Every path ranked on three axes — highest confidence surfaces first
Not every community is equal. Chick scores each one across three dimensions before showing the founder a single result. Highest-confidence paths come first — not biggest audiences.

Outreach Kit
The right move, for that specific place
The wrong approach in the right place wastes the shot. For each community, Chick drafts the move that matches the culture: lurk-and-comment for a subreddit, a direct DM for a Discord, an open post for a Facebook group. Not generic advice. The exact thing to say, in that format, for that community.
What Chick generates per community
7-day day-by-day plan · First post (copy-paste ready) · DM template · Platform-specific Do's & Don'ts · Week 1 KPIs · Insider tip from pattern data

5
🚀
Execute
Founder runs it — copy, paste, post, DM
The founder follows the plan. Posts the question. Replies to every comment. DMs the warm leads. Books the calls. Chick gave them the script and the right place to use it. Now they run it.
What the founder does
Day 1: lurk and observe. Day 2–4: comment and add value. Day 5: post. Day 6: DM warm contacts. Day 7: count conversations and calls booked.
5–12 post replies

6
📈
Tracking
Every attempt logged — outcome, channel, message
Every attempt gets logged: which channel, which message, which outcome (yes / no / interested / silence). This turns "I tried stuff and it didn't work" into structured data instead of a vague memory of failure.
What Chick tracks
Which posts got replies · Which DMs got responses · Which communities converted · Message variant performance · Time-to-response per platform · Silence rate (a signal too)

🔁
Refinement Loop — patterns emerge, confidence re-ranks
Every reply and every silence gets classified. Patterns emerge: the message tests well in Discords but dies in cold DMs. Or the audience guess was wrong entirely. This re-ranks confidence scoring for the next pass — and at scale becomes a cross-founder data moat. Every loop, the founder gets closer to product-market fit. Every loop, Chick's pattern intelligence gets sharper for every founder who comes after.
